# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

FolderOrganizer aims to provide a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone participating in the project.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- provide constructive technical feedback;
- focus discussions on the project and the problem being solved;
- respect differing experience levels and viewpoints;
- accept reasonable maintainership decisions and review feedback;
- avoid sharing another person's private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, spam, and other behavior that makes participation unsafe or hostile are not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces including issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and other official FolderOrganizer community interactions.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer privately rather than escalating the situation publicly.

Reports will be reviewed in context and handled as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.
